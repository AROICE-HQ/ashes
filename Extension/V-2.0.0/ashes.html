Updates

Version 2.5.1 "Afterglow"

Leaving Ashes is a real choice, and it deserves better than a silent disappearance. Uninstalling now offers a quick, optional "what didn't work" — takes ten seconds, and it's the only way this actually gets better for the next person.

Version 2.5.0 "Ember"

A trust pass. Digging into how the countdown was actually calculated turned up a handful of Life Factors questions that were quietly being ignored — your answers were collected but never changed your number. Fixed those, fixed a crash some people were hitting when selecting "Other" for gender, and gave former smokers credit for having quit instead of scoring them like current smokers. The countdown itself is also a bit more precise now, and the extension is a little harder to poke at from the outside.

Version 2.0.0

The biggest step up since launch. Your countdown is no longer just birthdate and a guess — it now factors in country, BMI, exercise, diet, sleep, stress, and more, so the number actually reflects you. A new Life Factors page lets you tune all of it, onboarding walks new users through setup properly, and everything still stays on your device. Nothing about how Ashes handles your data changed.

Version 1.3.1

Keyboard shortcuts moved to Chrome's own settings page, plus some smaller fixes along the way.

Version 1.3.0

A round of polish — the interface got cleaner, a few new ways to customize things showed up, and things generally feel smoother.

Version 1.2.0

Added a proper options page, so themes and display preferences no longer had to be set once and forgotten.
