v2.5.0 "Ember" Current August 17, 2026

An accuracy and security pass — the calculator now scores exactly what the form asks, and every page is a little harder to break.

  • Fixed several Life Factors questions that weren't affecting your calculated lifespan
  • Fixed a crash when selecting "Other" for gender
  • More accurate scoring for former smokers vs. current smokers
  • More precise countdown timing
  • Tightened security and privacy protections throughout

The biggest update yet — a truly personalized, science-informed life expectancy experience.

  • Advanced life expectancy calculator factoring country, age, gender, BMI, exercise, diet, sleep, stress, and more
  • New Life Factors page to fine-tune 30+ health and lifestyle variables, updating your countdown in real time
  • Guided, step-by-step onboarding
  • Personalized health recommendations based on your profile
  • Keyboard shortcuts now managed directly through Chrome's extension settings
  • Enhanced UI and accessibility, improved mobile support
  • Privacy-first: all data stays 100% local, nothing ever leaves your device
