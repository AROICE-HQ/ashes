Boilerplate

Ashes is a browser extension that replaces the default new tab page with a live countdown of the user's remaining time, calculated from their birthdate and a personalized life expectancy estimate. Built by Aryan Techie under AROICE, it's available for Chrome and Firefox.

One-liner:

A memento mori for your browser tabs.
